# People, Groups, and Roles Specification

Updated 2026-11-26 (follow-up 13 Phase 20): the screens as built by beads `d8l5.63`, `d8l5.61`, `d8l5.62` and `6wz1.110`, with the G2 pick OD-10-3 that the owner approved on 2026-11-18 and the owner words of 2026-11-14 on bead `6wz1.110`.

## Overview
The admin portal screens where a tenant administrator decides what people can do. People lists everyone who can sign in, with a right slide-over that shows one person's profile, groups, role assignments, and sessions. Groups shows directory groups read-only and groups the administrator manages. Roles defines named sets of permission keys, and nothing else: who holds a role is decided on the Access screen (`../access/spec.md`) and with Add assignment on a person or group sheet, all through the one role-assignment service (`DEC-39`). Add person offers no role (`d8l5.63`). Every list of assignments in this section links to Access.

## User Flows
- Administrator opens People, searches by name or email, filters by status (active, pending, suspended, deactivated) or group, and clicks a row to open the person inspector on the right without leaving the list.
- Administrator adds a person in a dialog with the work email, optionally a display name and, for a tenant-wide groups manager, groups made here. The dialog offers no role. After the save the new person's Roles tab opens, where roles are added (`d8l5.63`). The person appears as Pending until first sign-in. When local accounts are on, the dialog says that a set-password email will be sent. When the new person's account type is brokered (always in client-only mode), the dialog carries "Send invitation email", checked by default; the email carries the sign-in link (Specification 02 R-40a). A pending brokered person's Profile tab offers Resend invitation with the last-sent time.
- Administrator adds a directory group before anyone in it signs in, by typing the exact value the identity provider sends (for example an Entra group object ID), and assigns roles at once. The group shows "Not seen yet" until a sign-in lists it, and it can be deleted while it is not seen (`DEC-52`).
- Administrator gives a directory group a readable label, for example when the provider sends object IDs. Lists show the label; the group inspector shows the label and the value (Specification 02 R-24c).
- Administrator suspends a person (they cannot sign in but keep history), unsuspends them, or deactivates them, each with a confirm step. Deactivating ends every session, bans the person, and drops their group memberships and direct role assignments; their name, email, and audit trail stay as they are. Personal erasure (the anonymized name) is a separate operator command, `genie-ops erase`, and never a screen action (DEC-14, DEC-17).
- Administrator cannot suspend, deactivate, or strip roles from their own row; those controls are disabled with a tooltip. No action may leave zero active holders of the Tenant administrator role (direct or through a group): the row menu Suspend and Deactivate, a direct assignment's remove, the last assignment in Role detail, deleting the administrators group, archiving a directory group that carries the role, removing all its members, or removing one of its members. Each such control is disabled with the tooltip "This would leave no active tenant administrator"; the server refuses too.
- The rule counts people, never assignment rows. Only a person whose status is active counts: a pending person has not signed in yet and a suspended person cannot sign in, so neither can administer the tenant today. A path through an archived group counts for nobody, because an archived group keeps its assignments and grants nothing. Two paths to the same person are still one person. Access applies the same rule to its own writes (`../access/spec.md`).
- Administrator opens a pending local-account person and presses Resend set-password email; the realm sends it and the row shows when it was last sent. There is no manual activation; first sign-in activates. A person's account type (brokered or local) is fixed at creation: a local account created while local accounts were on stays local after the setting is turned off, so this block can appear in a tenant whose settings show local accounts off. Editing a local account's email (administrator only, audited) is out of scope for this round.
- Administrator sorts People by name, status, or last sign-in, and follows a group chip on a person straight into that group's inspector.
- Administrator opens the Sessions tab of a person and signs out one session or all of them.
- Administrator opens the Groups tab of a person: directory groups are read-only with "Last synced <time>", and group membership can be added or removed.
- Administrator opens the Roles tab of a person: the standard assignments table lists each role with its scope (All records or the record), Assigned via (Direct or "<group> (group)"), and "Assigned <date> by <name>" under the role. A tenant-wide roles manager gets Add assignment. A people manager sees View only. Your own account shows no actions. Manage in Access opens the Access Overview for the person.
- Administrator opens Groups, sees directory groups and groups with member counts, and opens a group inspector. The card under the header answers what the group reaches, with a count. Manage in Access sits under the Roles table. A directory group shows members and "Last synced <time>" read-only. A group allows editing name and description and adding or removing members in bulk through a transfer list.
- Administrator sees a directory group that no longer arrives in the token marked Missing from directory with its last-seen time. It keeps its role assignments and can be archived, not deleted. Archive is a header action on every directory group, missing from the directory or not.
- Administrator deletes a group after a confirm step that names how many members and how many role assignments go with it, or removes all its members at once from the Members tab.
- Administrator reads the roles a group carries in the group inspector, follows a link to the role, or follows Manage in Access to change what the group reaches.
- Administrator sees that the Tenant administrator built-in role already carries the admin permission of every module that is on, marked "Added automatically for administrators", so no role edit is needed to administer a new module.
- Administrator opens Roles, sees built-in roles seeded by modules and custom roles, and creates a custom role with a name, description, and permission keys picked from a list grouped by module. Built-in roles can be copied with Copy as new role but not edited or deleted.
- Administrator opens a role and reads What this role allows, grouped by module with "Module on" or "Module off" for each module, and Show keys shows the permission keys. Keys of a module that is off carry a warning.
- Administrator opens a role and reads "Assigned to" with a count: the first 10 assignments, groups first, then by name, each a link to the person or the group with "Group · n members" or "Person · Direct". Past 10, "See all <n> assignments" opens the Access Overview by role, as Manage in Access does (`d8l5.47`, which reverses LK8). A role is never assigned from this screen.

## UI Requirements
- Renders in the admin portal chrome. Three pages: People, Groups, Roles, each with a page title, one-line description, a search field (with a help icon beside it on Groups and Roles), and a primary action on the right (Add person, New group, New role). Groups also carries a secondary action, Add directory group, which asks for the exact claim value, an optional label, and roles.
- People toolbar: the search field, a sort select, a Filters button holding Status and Group, and the Add person primary action. The button carries the count of active filters, active filters render as removable chips below the toolbar with a Clear filters, and the no-match row carries Clear filters. Sort stays in the row, because it orders the list and never removes one (`../../design-system/tokens.md`, Filter disclosure). Groups carries one Show archived checkbox and Roles carries none, so neither takes a Filters button.
- People table columns: person (avatar initials, name, email), status pill (Active green, Pending gray, Suspended red, Deactivated red), groups (first two as pills plus a count), roles count, last sign-in. Row click opens the inspector. Row overflow menu holds Suspend or Unsuspend, and Deactivate. On phone width the table is a card list with name, email, status pill, and last sign-in. The break-glass account never appears in People, Groups, or Roles; it is visible only in the audit log.
- Inspector: a right slide-over about 480px wide with the person's header (avatar, name, email, status pill, identity source) and tabs Profile, Groups, Roles, Sessions. Escape or the close button closes it. On small screens it becomes a full-height sheet.
- Profile tab: name, email, account type, identity source (the identity provider, or "Genie Ops Center (local password)" for a local account), first sign-in, last sign-in, onboarding state, with Suspend or Unsuspend and Deactivate buttons at the bottom. The set-password block appears only for a pending local account.
- Groups tab: two lists. Directory groups read-only with a "Last synced <time>" line and a note that membership is managed in the identity provider. Groups with an Add to group picker and a remove control per row.
- Roles tab: the heading "Assigned roles (n)", the help How access adds up, then the standard assignments table: Role, Scope (All records or the record) and Assigned via (Direct or "<group> (group)"), with "Assigned <date> by <name>" under the role, then Manage in Access. A tenant-wide roles manager gets Add assignment. A viewer without `core:roles:manage` sees View only and no Manage in Access (`d8l5.43`). Your own account shows no actions.
- Sessions tab: same table pattern as the account page, with Sign out per row and Sign out all.
- Add person dialog: work email (required), display name (optional), and, for a tenant-wide groups manager, groups made here (optional), and a note about Pending. It has no roles multi-select. After the save it opens the new person's Roles tab (`d8l5.63`, Specification 02 R-40). When local accounts are on, an account type choice (identity provider or local password) and, for local, a note that the set-password email is sent. Errors inline.
  Amended 2026-11-26 (`d8l5.63`, OD-10-3): the role choices of 2026-11-08 (`d8l5.44`, `add-person.prototype.html` variant A) are gone. Roles are added after the save, on the new person's Roles tab.
- Groups table columns: group (name, description), source pill (Directory, Local), members count, roles count, "Last synced <time>" or a Missing from directory badge with the last-seen time. Archived groups hidden behind a Show archived toggle. Row click opens the group inspector.
- Group inspector, top to bottom: a header that carries identity only (icon, name, source pill, description, and the synced or local line), one access card, the Members and Roles tabs, the tab content, and one action bar at the foot of the sheet. A directory group that is missing from the directory shows its banner between the header and the access card.
- Group inspector access card: the title "Access is managed in one place" and the count, "<n> roles assigned via this group". Manage in Access sits under the Roles table and opens the Access Overview for the group. The card shows on both tabs, because what a group reaches is the first question a group raises.
- Group inspector action bar: a group carries Edit name and description, which swaps the header for an inline form, and Delete group. A directory group carries Rename and Archive group, and one line that says the value, the description, and the members come from the identity provider, which is why only the name is editable. A not-yet-seen directory group carries Delete group instead of Archive group. An archived group carries no action and one line that says its assignments are kept and give nothing while it is archived. Archive is disabled with the reason when the group is the last active path to Tenant administrator, because archiving stops the group granting. The last-administrator reason sits above the bar when Delete or Archive is blocked.
- Group inspector Members tab: a group uses the member transfer list in its roster form, with Remove all at the right of the Members heading. A directory group shows a read-only list and the directory count line.
- Removing members from a group confirms first. The dialog names how many people leave, and it says that their direct roles and their other group memberships still give access and that this does not remove them. Adding members does not confirm. Remove all carries the same sentence.
- Contextual help (design system, Help disclosure): one collapsed button per screen. The group inspector carries How group access works under the access card, on both tabs. The person inspector carries How access adds up on its Roles tab. The Roles directory carries How roles work as an icon beside the search field, and it says that a built-in role is read-only and copyable, that a custom role is editable, and that Access gives a role to a person or a group. The Groups directory carries Groups and directory groups as an icon beside its search field, and it says that a directory group's value and members come from the identity provider and only its name is editable here, that a group not seen yet can be deleted, that both kinds carry roles, and that a group missing from the directory is archived rather than deleted. Role detail carries Why does it include module admin? on the Tenant administrator role. Each opens on click, on tap, and on Enter or Space, and Escape closes it.
- Roles table columns: role (name, description), kind pill (Built-in or Custom), permissions count, assignments count. Row click opens the role detail page.
- Role detail with no assignments reads "It has no assignments." (copy kept as written, 6wz1.82 and L303, 2026-10-28).
- Role detail: header with name, description, kind, and actions (Edit for custom, Copy as new role for built-in). The kind reads "Built-in role" or "Custom role". Two blocks: What this role allows, grouped by module with a pill per module (Module on green or Module off gray), a Show keys switch, and a warning icon on keys of a module that is off. On the Tenant administrator role, each module admin key carries "Added automatically for administrators". A one-record assignment carries the record label alone as its pill, for example "INV-1042".
- Person inspector, Roles tab: Assigned via distinguishes "Direct" and "<group> (group)", so an administrator can see why they reach a module admin screen.
- Add person dialog: in `invite` mode the note reads "Pending until first sign-in"; in `jit` mode it reads "People in a group mapped to a role can also sign in without being added here" (`DEC-7` as amended 2026-09-27).
- Designed mobile first; phone layout is the base and the desktop layout adds columns. Every table is a card list under 768px with the row actions in an overflow menu. Assigned to list: icon, name and "Person · Direct" or "Group · n members", read-only, with Manage in Access.
- Role form: name, description, and a permission picker grouped by module with a checkbox per key and a select-all per module. Used for New role, Edit, and Copy as new role (prefilled).
- Role form: the name must be unique among roles; a duplicate is flagged inline.
- Every destructive action (Suspend, Deactivate, Sign out all sessions, delete group, archive directory group, remove all members, delete a role) opens the confirm dialog from the design system: the title names the object, one sentence of consequence, Cancel focused, danger tone. Unsuspend does not confirm. Phone primary actions (Add person, New group, New role) sit in the shell's bottom bar.
- Tokens: blue for primary actions, links, and active tabs, semantic pills (emerald active or module on, gray pending or neutral, red suspended or down, amber missing from directory), gray neutrals, rounded-xl cards, rounded-lg controls, Plus Jakarta Sans, JetBrains Mono for identifiers such as permission keys.
- Out of scope: a Groups screen state for a sign-in whose token carried no groups claim (memberships kept, `auth:groups_claim_absent` written, `DEC-41`); the audit reader shows the event and nothing on Groups changes. Also out of scope: granting or revoking access, which the Access section owns, editing directory group membership, password or authenticator management, editing a local account's email, bulk import, and SCIM.
