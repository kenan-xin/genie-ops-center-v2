# People, Groups, and Roles Specification

## Overview
The admin portal screens where a tenant administrator decides what people can do. People lists everyone who can sign in, with a right slide-over that shows one person's profile, groups, role assignments, and sessions. Groups shows identity-provider groups read-only and local groups the administrator manages. Roles defines named sets of permission keys, and nothing else: who holds a role is decided on the Access screen (`../access/spec.md`), which is the one writer of role assignments (`DEC-39`). Every list of assignments in this section is read-only and links there.

## User Flows
- Administrator opens People, searches by name or email, filters by status (active, pending, disabled) or group, and clicks a row to open the person inspector on the right without leaving the list.
- Administrator adds a person by email in a dialog, optionally with a display name and roles to assign now. The person appears as Pending until first sign-in. When local accounts are on, the dialog says that a set-password email will be sent.
- Administrator disables a person (they cannot sign in but keep history), re-enables them, or removes them, each with a confirm step. Removing ends every session, bans the person, and drops their group memberships and direct role assignments; their name, email, and audit trail stay as they are. Personal erasure (the anonymized name) is a separate operator command, `genie-ops erase`, and never a screen action (DEC-14, DEC-17).
- Administrator cannot disable, remove, or strip roles from their own row; those controls are disabled with a tooltip. No action may leave zero active holders of the Tenant administrator role (direct or through a group): the row menu Disable and Remove, a direct assignment's remove, the last assignment in Role detail, deleting the administrators group, removing all its members, or removing one of its members. Each such control is disabled with the tooltip "This is the last tenant administrator"; the server refuses too.
- Administrator opens a pending local-account person and presses Resend set-password email; the realm sends it and the row shows when it was last sent. There is no manual activation; first sign-in activates. A person's account type (brokered or local) is fixed at creation: a local account created while local accounts were on stays local after the setting is turned off, so this block can appear in a tenant whose settings show local accounts off. Editing a local account's email (administrator only, audited) is out of scope for this round.
- Administrator sorts People by name, status, or last sign-in, and follows a group chip on a person straight into that group's inspector.
- Administrator opens the Sessions tab of a person and signs out one session or all of them.
- Administrator opens the Groups tab of a person: identity-provider groups are read-only with a synced-at time, local group membership can be added or removed.
- Administrator opens the Roles tab of a person: role assignments are listed with their scope and how they arrive (direct or through a group). The list is read-only, and Manage in Access opens `/admin/access?recipient=<id>` with the person already chosen.
- Administrator opens Groups, sees directory and local groups with member counts, and opens a group inspector. The card under the header answers what the group reaches, with a count and one link into Access. A directory group shows members and last sync read-only. A local group allows editing name and description and adding or removing members in bulk through a transfer list.
- Administrator sees a directory group that no longer arrives in the token marked as stale with its last-seen time. It keeps its role assignments and can be archived, not deleted. Archive is a header action on every directory group, stale or not.
- Administrator deletes a local group after a confirm step that names how many members and how many role assignments go with it, or removes all its members at once from the Members tab.
- Administrator reads the roles a group carries in the group inspector, follows a link to the role, or follows Manage in Access to change what the group reaches.
- Administrator sees that the Tenant administrator system role already carries every entitled module's admin permission, added automatically when the module was entitled, so no role edit is needed to administer a new module.
- Administrator opens Roles, sees system roles seeded by modules and custom roles, and creates a custom role with a name, description, and permission keys picked from a list grouped by module. System roles can be copied but not edited or deleted.
- Administrator opens a role and reads its permission keys grouped by module with each module's entitlement state. Keys of a module the tenant is not entitled to carry a warning.
- Administrator opens a role and reads who holds it, at which scope. The list is read-only, and Manage in Access opens the Grants screen. A role is never assigned from this screen.

## UI Requirements
- Renders in the admin portal chrome. Three pages: People, Groups, Roles, each with a page title, one-line description, a search field, and a primary action on the right (Add person, New local group, New role).
- People table columns: person (avatar initials, name, email), status pill (Active green, Pending gray, Disabled red), groups (first two as pills plus a count), roles count, last sign-in. Row click opens the inspector. Row overflow menu holds Disable or Re-enable and Remove. On phone width the table is a card list with name, email, status pill, and last sign-in. The break-glass account never appears in People, Groups, or Roles; it is visible only in the audit log.
- Inspector: a right slide-over about 480px wide with the person's header (avatar, name, email, status pill, identity source) and tabs Profile, Groups, Roles, Sessions. Escape or the close button closes it. On small screens it becomes a full-height sheet.
- Profile tab: name, email, account type, identity source (the identity provider, or "Genie (local password)" for a local account), first sign-in, last sign-in, onboarding state, with Disable or Re-enable and Remove buttons at the bottom. The set-password block appears only for a pending local account.
- Groups tab: two lists. Directory groups read-only with a synced-at line and a note that membership is managed in the identity provider. Local groups with an Add to group picker and a remove control per row.
- Roles tab: read-only list of assignments with role name, module, scope (Whole tenant or the record label), and source (Direct or via group name), then one Manage in Access button and one line that says a role held through a group changes for every member of that group.
- Sessions tab: same table pattern as the account page, with Sign out per row and Sign out all.
- Add person dialog: email (required), display name (optional), roles multi-select (optional), a note about Pending. When local accounts are on, an account type choice (identity provider or local password) and, for local, a note that the set-password email is sent. Errors inline.
- Groups table columns: group (name, description), source pill (Directory, Local), members count, roles count, last sync or stale badge with last-seen time. Archived groups hidden behind a Show archived toggle. Row click opens the group inspector.
- Group inspector, top to bottom: a header that carries identity only (icon, name, source pill, description, and the synced or local line), one access card, the Members and Roles tabs, the tab content, and one action bar at the foot of the sheet. A stale directory group shows its banner between the header and the access card.
- Group inspector access card: the title "Access is managed in one place", the count of grants through this group, and Open in Access, which opens `/admin/access?recipient=<id>`. The whole card is the control, and it shows on both tabs, because what a group reaches is the first question a group raises.
- Group inspector action bar: a local group carries Edit name and description, which swaps the header for an inline form, and Delete group. A directory group carries Archive group and one line that says the name, the description, and the members come from the identity provider, which is why nothing there is editable. An archived group carries no action and one line that says its assignments are kept and give nothing while it is archived. The last-administrator reason sits above the bar when Delete is blocked.
- Group inspector Members tab: a local group uses the member transfer list in its roster form, with Remove all at the right of the Members heading. A directory group shows a read-only list and the directory count line.
- Removing members from a local group confirms first. The dialog names how many people leave, and it says that their direct roles and their other group memberships still give access and that this does not remove them. Adding members does not confirm. Remove all carries the same sentence.
- Contextual help (design system, Help disclosure): one collapsed button per screen. The group inspector carries How group access works under the access card, on both tabs. The person inspector carries How this adds up on its Roles tab. The Roles directory carries How roles work beside the search field, and it says that a system role is read-only and copyable, that a custom role is editable, and that Access gives a role to a person or a group. The People directory carries Pending, disabled, removed beside its search field, and it says that Pending ends at first sign-in, what Disable keeps and what Remove drops, and that replacing a name with an anonymous one is an operator command. The Groups directory carries Directory and local groups beside its search field, and it says that a directory group is read-only here, that both kinds carry roles, and that a stale group is archived rather than deleted. Role detail carries none: the entitlement warning per module and the note about keys added by entitlement already explain it in place. Each opens on click, on tap, and on Enter or Space, and Escape closes it.
- Roles table columns: role (name, description), kind pill (System with the module name, or Custom), permissions count, assignments count. Row click opens the role detail page.
- Role detail: header with name, description, kind, and actions (Edit for custom, Copy for system). Two blocks: Permissions grouped by module with an entitlement pill per module (Entitled green or Not entitled gray) and a warning icon on keys of a non-entitled module. The Tenant administrator role shows a note that a module's admin permission is appended automatically when the module is entitled, and each such key carries an "Added by entitlement" marker.
- Person inspector, Roles tab: the source line distinguishes "Direct", "via <group>", and "via <group> as Tenant administrator (system)" so an administrator can see why they reach a module admin screen.
- Add person dialog: in `invite` mode the note reads "Pending until first sign-in"; in `jit` mode it reads "People in your identity provider can also sign in without being added here".
- Designed mobile first; phone layout is the base and the desktop layout adds columns. Every table is a card list under 768px with the row actions in an overflow menu. Assignments table with principal (person or group), scope, added by, and added on, read-only, with one Manage in Access button in its header.
- Role form: name, description, and a permission picker grouped by module with a checkbox per key and a select-all per module. Used for New role, Edit, and Copy (prefilled).
- Role form: the name must be unique among roles; a duplicate is flagged inline.
- Every destructive action (Disable, Remove person, Sign out all sessions, delete local group, archive directory group, remove all members, delete a role) opens the confirm dialog from the design system: the title names the object, one sentence of consequence, Cancel focused, danger tone. Re-enable does not confirm. Phone primary actions (Add person, New local group, New role) sit in the shell's bottom bar.
- Tokens: blue for primary actions, links, and active tabs, semantic pills (emerald active or entitled, gray pending or neutral, red disabled or down, amber stale), gray neutrals, rounded-xl cards, rounded-lg controls, Plus Jakarta Sans, JetBrains Mono for identifiers such as permission keys.
- Out of scope: a Groups screen state for a sign-in whose token carried no groups claim (memberships kept, `auth:groups_claim_absent` written, `DEC-41`); the audit reader shows the event and nothing on Groups changes. Also out of scope: granting or revoking access, which the Access section owns, editing directory group membership, password or authenticator management, editing a local account's email, bulk import, and SCIM.
