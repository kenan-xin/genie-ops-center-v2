PROTOTYPE: review desk
Hover or click a field: its line lights up on the document.

A. Polished

  • Keeps the floating cards with leader lines and the "Before you save" panel.
  • Fixes: 32 px fields, chips only on fields that need you, 24 px panel padding, Save says "Save".
  • Phone keeps the step wizard, now 7 steps with a progress bar and the zoomed line of the document.
  • Cost: two places to look (cards and panel); the wizard hides the other fields; leader lines get busy on long invoices.

B. One form

  • Document on the left, one form on the right: what needs you first, then the fields from the document, then optional ones.
  • Each field has a locate button; hover or focus lights its line on the document. No leader lines.
  • Phone: the same form under a document preview; tap the preview to open it full screen. No wizard.
  • Matches Dext, Ramp and Bill.com. Cost: loses the "cards pinned to the page" picture of today.
