PROTOTYPE: Edit slide

What changed from the build

  • No holes: the form stacks from the top with one 20 px gap. The build's body is a grid that fills the sheet, so its rows stretched apart.
  • A live preview of the slide at the top shows each change before Save, as slide tools do.
  • The header names the slide ("Slide 4 of 5") and closes with X. The odd line "Its stored figures stay as they are" is gone; the charts section says it plainly instead.
  • The title wraps on two lines, so the whole sentence is visible while you edit it. Title and Text show a character count against the limit (90, 280), so a long title is never a surprise at Save.
  • "Charts and images" is one titled list with a count ("1 of 2"). Each row has a thumbnail, its name, its kind and source, and a labelled Remove icon button.
  • Charts come from the agent: one line says so and links to the Slides agent, instead of leaving the user to guess why a chart has no fields.
  • Add image is a left-aligned secondary button with an icon and the accepted types. On a full slide it stays visible but disabled, with the reason under it.
  • An image row carries its required Alt text field right under it.
  • Text or bullets: one body block. With none, two buttons "Add text" and "Add bullets". With text, a "Use bullets" link switches the kind and keeps the words.
  • Footer: Cancel and Save slide, right-aligned on desktop, two equal buttons on a phone. The phone sheet is the house bottom sheet with a grab handle.

Words

  • "Charts and images", "Remove", "Add image", "Alt text", "Use bullets", "Use text": plain, and the same in the Add element menu.
