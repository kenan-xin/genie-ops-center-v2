Demo policy & boundaries
  • SGD only. Meals: SGD 80 per receipt. Transport: SGD 60 per receipt.
  • A missing receipt date must be completed before review.
  • An over-limit claim needs a reviewer note. Review never removes the warning.
  • The same sample file is kept once. Similar-looking receipts are not detected.
  • “Reviewed” is not approval, reimbursement or proof that a claim is genuine.
